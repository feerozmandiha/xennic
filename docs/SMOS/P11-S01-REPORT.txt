3. KEY FEATURES PER PLAYBOOK
================================================================================

OPS-000 — Foundation
  - Purpose & scope, team roles & RACI, operating principles (10 principles)
  - Content lifecycle: brief → production → review → publish → analyze → improve
  - Daily/weekly/monthly cadences with decision tables
  - Inputs/outputs, success criteria, failure conditions
  - Review cycle, continuous improvement, document ownership

OPS-002 — Social Media Operations
  - 8 platform profiles with content roles & limits
  - Daily publishing workflow with checklists and decision tables
  - Cross-platform coordination (Hub → Platform mirror strategy)
  - Crisis communication protocol
  - Tool stack: n8n, Figma, designer handoff

OPS-004 — Editorial Calendar
  - Calendar structure: quarterly themes, monthly campaigns, weekly schedule
  - Priority assignment (P0-P3), status tracking (7 states)
  - Capacity planning formula, theme/campaign tracking
  - Publishing schedule templates, content pillars

OPS-006 — Content Production
  - Brief-to-final 5-stage workflow with quality gates
  - Research & sourcing standards
  - File naming conventions, revision management
  - AI tool integration guidelines
  - Production exit criteria checklist

OPS-008 — Visual Design System
  - Design request intake process with forms & SLAs
  - File naming conventions, brand colors operational guide
  - Template management, export/delivery specs, tool integrations
  - Quality checklist: brand compliance, typography, color accuracy, etc.

OPS-010 — Brand Voice
  - Writing style checklist (14 items), tone matrix per platform (10 combos)
  - Vocabulary: allowed words, forbidden words, ambiguous terms
  - CTA library with tone variants, common mistakes section
  - Voice compliance review, monthly brand voice audit

OPS-012 — Community Management
  - Response SLAs (P0-P3), comment triage process
  - Escalation protocols, crisis response decision tree
  - Moderation guidelines: delete, hide, reply, escalate
  - Sentiment monitoring, weekly report structure

OPS-014 — Content Quality Checklist
  - 6 quality gates with weighted scoring (0-100)
  - Detailed checklists: structural, grammar, brand, accuracy, visual,
    platform, SEO, accessibility, link, legal
  - Quality gate decision table (pass/conditional/fail)
  - Continuous improvement cycle

OPS-016 — Publishing Checklist
  - Pre-publishing, platform-specific, post-publishing checks
  - 6 platform-specific checks (Instagram, LinkedIn, Telegram/Bale,
    X/Twitter, YouTube/Aparat, Website)
  - Formatting, media attachment, URL/hashtag/mention verification
  - Timezone cop (Tehran UTC+3:30), rollback procedure
  - Publishing log template

OPS-018 — KPI Review Playbook
  - 3-level dashboard: tactical (daily), operational (weekly), strategic (monthly)
  - Anomaly detection framework, cross-platform comparison
  - Audience growth analysis, sentiment review, competitor benchmarking
  - Alert thresholds for all metrics
  - Quarterly KPI review meeting structure
