  - Mirror Pipeline: Aparat mirrors YouTube long-form videos

RISKS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  R-01  API Limitation: Aparat REST API has limited capabilities.
        Manual or semi-automated upload may be required.
        Mitigation: Hybrid pipeline (API + manual fallback).

  R-02  Platform Stability: Aparat is a domestic platform with
        potential service interruptions or policy changes.
        Mitigation: YouTube remains primary source.

  R-03  Engagement Low: Iranian audience engagement on Aparat
        is typically lower than YouTube.
        Mitigation: Focus on accessibility, not engagement metrics.

  R-04  Content Compliance: Iranian content regulations may
        require filtering or modification.
        Mitigation: Compliance Gate (Iran) in Quality Gates.

RECOMMENDATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Prioritize Mirror Completeness (KPI-PLAT-006-06) as the
     primary success metric for the first 3 months.

  2. Establish Mirror Pipeline (AUT-006-PUB) before manual uploads.

  3. Invest in Persian SEO keyword research — Aparat's search
     algorithm differs significantly from YouTube.

  4. Monitor Iran-specific analytics monthly — Aparat's analytics
     capabilities are limited, manual sampling may be needed.

  5. Keep Iran-exclusive content as a secondary priority —
     mirror completeness is the core value proposition.
