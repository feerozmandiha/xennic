2. PLATFORM ARCHITECTURE SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The multi-platform architecture is organized in three layers:

  Strategic Layer:
    BRD-001 (Brand Identity) → EDT-001 (Content OS) → EDT-001 (Editorial)
    These define WHAT and WHY for all platforms.

  Orchestration Layer:
    Canonical Content Hub → Knowledge Repository → Scheduling
    This layer manages the HOW of cross-platform operation.

  Distribution Layer:
    7 platforms + Future Platforms — each with a defined enterprise role.

  Knowledge Flow:
    Every platform returns engagement data → Analytics → Insights →
    Knowledge Repository → Canonical Content improvement.


3. PLATFORM CLASSIFICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Platforms are classified across 4 dimensions:
  Ownership (Owned vs Third-Party)
  Content Nature (Text, Image, Video, Mixed)
  Communication Nature (Broadcast, Community, Network)
  Audience (General, Professional, Niche)

Classification mapping:
  Platform         | Ownership   | Content     | Communication | Audience
  -----------------|-------------|-------------|---------------|---------
  Website / Blog   | Owned       | Mixed       | Broadcast     | General
  Instagram        | Third-Party | Mixed       | Network       | General
  LinkedIn         | Third-Party | Mixed       | Network       | Professional
  Telegram         | Third-Party | Mixed       | Community     | General
  Bale             | Third-Party | Mixed       | Community     | General
  YouTube          | Third-Party | Video       | Broadcast     | General
  Aparat           | Third-Party | Video       | Broadcast     | General (Iran)
  Future Platforms | Variable    | Variable    | Variable      | Variable

Strategic roles assigned:
  - Hub (Website/Blog): Central repository, SSOT for publication
  - Reach (Instagram, YouTube): Broadest audience reach
  - Network (LinkedIn): Professional & B2B connections
  - Community (Telegram, Bale): Direct engagement & dialogue
  - Archive (YouTube, Aparat): Long-term video storage

Priority tiers:
  P0 — Hub (Website/Blog)          25% resource allocation
  P1 — Primary (Instagram, LinkedIn, Telegram)  45%
  P2 — Secondary (Bale, YouTube, Aparat)  20%
  P3 — Experimental (Future)       10%


4. AUDIENCE ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7-stage audience journey defined:
  Awareness → Attention → Engagement → Trust → Conversion → Loyalty → Advocacy

Each stage has primary and secondary platform assignments.
Example: Awareness maps to Instagram (primary) and LinkedIn (secondary).
Example: Conversion maps to Website (primary) and Telegram (secondary).

5 audience journey principles (AJ-01 to AJ-05) govern movement between stages.


5. KNOWLEDGE FLOW SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6 types of knowledge return from platforms:
  - Engagement Pattern → Platform optimization
  - Audience Insight → Better audience understanding
  - Content Performance → Quality improvement
  - Trend Signal → New trend identification
  - Competitive Intel → Competitive analysis
  - Feedback & Sentiment → Product/service improvement

Complete feedback loop: Content Hub → Distribution Hub → Platforms →
Analytics → Knowledge Repository → Content Hub

8. RISKS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Strategic risks identified and mitigated:
  1. Third-party platform dependency (HIGH) → Owned Hub, multi-platform strategy
  2. Algorithm changes (MEDIUM) → Content-centric, not algorithm-centric
  3. Sanctions/filtering (HIGH) → Iranian alternatives (Bale, Aparat) as backup
  4. API policy changes (HIGH) → Layered architecture, isolated integration
  5. Audience fatigue (MEDIUM) → Content diversity, platform-specific localization
  6. Cross-platform conflict (LOW) → Roles, cross-posting rules, clear boundaries
