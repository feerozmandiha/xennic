6. ARCHITECTURAL DECISIONS
--------------------------
- ADR-001: SMOS Constitution (CON-000) is the supreme document.
  All other documents are subordinate to it.
- ADR-002: Documents are classified into 8 tiers:
  Strategic, Platform, Automation, Agent, Operational, Reference, Training, Archive
- ADR-003: Prefix "WF" is deprecated in favor of "AUT" for automation workflows.
- ADR-004: Asset management (AST) is separated from platform playbooks.
- ADR-005: Knowledge base (KNW) is a reference tier, separate from operational.
- ADR-006: Reports (REP) are separated from Operations (OPS) for SSOT clarity.
- ADR-007: MCP servers added to audience metadata field.
- ADR-008: CON-000 uses MAJOR.MINOR versioning only (no PATCH).
- ADR-009: Status "superseded" added to metadata for replaced documents.
