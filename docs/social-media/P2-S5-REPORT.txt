Key Decisions
-------------
- TW-01 to TW-06: Core principles for X/Twitter — speed with accuracy
- News role defined as new ARCH-020 strategic role extension
- Character limits managed via post class (Flash/Short/Medium/Long)
- Thread architecture: 3-15 posts, numbered (X/Y), CTA in final post
- Crisis protocol in X: < 15 min response time, auto-hold publishing
- #Xennic branded hashtag required in all posts
- Quote (with comment) preferred over bare Retweet
- Breaking News priority over all other content types
- Trend Monitoring with Knowledge Capture every 4 hours
